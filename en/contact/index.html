Questions buyers ask

If your answer is not here, call our sales engineer directly.

+421 904 534 897
01What do you need for a quotation?

A PDF drawing, ideally with DXF, DWG or STEP, plus material, thickness, quantity and surface finish. If anything is missing, an engineer will get in touch.

02Do you make prototypes and small batches?

Yes. Prototypes, small, medium and large series as well as repeat orders.

03Will you sign an NDA before we send drawings?

Gladly. We do not develop competing products and use your documentation only for your order.

04Which materials do you process?

Steel, stainless steel, aluminium, galvanised sheet, copper and brass.

05Can you deliver a finished assembly instead of parts?

Yes. Welding, grinding, surface finishing, assembly and packing in one chain.

06Do you deliver abroad?

Yes, regularly to Austria, Czechia and Germany. Goods are packed to your requirements with export documents.

07When will I get a quotation?

Every request goes through an engineering review and we come back with a price or questions. The lead time is confirmed in the quotation.
